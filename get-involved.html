Get involved

There are many ways to support our mission and uplift the community.

Three ways to help

🙌

Volunteer

Share your time, talents, and heart by helping with outreach, events, and community support.

💛

Donate

Your financial contribution helps provide food, education support, and care for families in need.

🤝

Partner

We welcome collaborations with businesses, schools, and community organisations with aligned values.

How it works

Volunteering is free. Outreach happens on the first Saturday of each month at 08:00. Sign up.

Give any amount by EFT. A weekly food parcel for a family costs R250. Ask about donating.

Businesses and schools can sponsor events from R500. Ask about partnering.
