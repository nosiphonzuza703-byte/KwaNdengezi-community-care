Our programmes

🥘

Food security

We organise food parcels and meal support so families are not left without essentials.

Cost: From R250 per family parcel. Availability: Weekly, collect Tue and Thu.

👩‍🏫

Education support

We encourage learning, provide school-related support, and create spaces where children can thrive.

Cost: From R150 per school pack. Availability: Term time.

💬

Emotional support

Our outreach includes listening, encouragement, and connection with trusted care networks.

Cost: Free. Availability: By appointment.

🎨

Youth programmes

We create positive activities and leadership opportunities for young people.

Cost: From R100 per session. Availability: School holidays and weekends.

🩺

Health awareness

We help families access information and practical support that improves wellbeing.

Cost: Free. Availability: Monthly awareness days.

🤲

Community care

We strengthen local networks of care so people are supported in times of need.

Cost: Free or in-kind. Availability: First Saturday of each month.
